About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where people can stop in for dependable favorites and discover something new. Our team prepares bread, pastries, and cakes in small batches so the bakery feels personal and connected to the community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on dependable ingredients and thoughtful preparation. Whenever practical, we choose ingredients from regional suppliers and plan seasonal items around what is fresh and available.

Meet the Team

Our bakers and counter staff work together from early morning through closing to prepare products, help customers with selections, and coordinate pre-orders for special occasions.
